About Me

I'm a Year 2 IT student at Republic Polytechnic specialising in Full Stack Development, with a strong interest in building web and mobile applications that are both functional and visually polished. I take pride in the details, from how an interface looks to how it feels to use.

Outside of coding, I have a strong passion for sports, particularly football, which I play regularly. I also recently developed an interest in building Gaming PCs, starting with my own build and since then helping friends and family with theirs. My current goals are to build a solid foundation in software development, pursue a university degree, and continue working on meaningful projects. I'm exploring opportunities in software engineering, web development, and business data analytics, and in the long run I aspire to work at a top-tier tech company and see how far I can go in this industry.
