I'm Angelo, an Information Technology student who builds web apps from the first sketch to the live site.

I'm a Year 2 Information Technology student at Republic Polytechnic, majoring in Full Stack Development, and building for the web is what I enjoy most.

I love taking random ideas and turning them into real apps people can actually use, like a football quiz or a PC part picker. Getting it to work is only half of it for me. I want it to look good and feel smooth to use too, so I spend as much time on the design as I do on the code.

Outside of class, I'm usually building or fixing PCs. My own runs on a Ryzen 7 7800X3D, and getting it stable meant chasing blue screens, catching faulty RAM with MemTest86 and replacing a faulty CPU. Now I help friends and family put together their builds, and one day I'd like to turn it into a small side business. When I'm not doing that, I'm playing football.

Right now, I'm looking for a web development internship from March 2027, where I can build real products with a team and keep getting better.
